Quality interpretation

Observed values, partial lower bounds, unsupported methods, collection failures, and genuinely missing facts remain distinct. Daily quality and coverage use the selected date window. TVL, depth, and execution use independently timestamped latest snapshots.

Hover or focus a daily status to inspect its cause and affected UTC dates. Retryable gaps are routed to a protected operator queue; confirmed no-candle, listing, and source-history outcomes require review rather than an automatic retry.
